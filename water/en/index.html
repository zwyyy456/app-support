A simple daily counter

One tap. One less thing to remember.

Log water and standing with your usual preset amounts. Turn on the optional kegel counter in Settings when you want it. See today's progress as soon as you open the app.

How it works

Made for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Sync across your own devices with your private iCloud database.

Keep daily tracking simple

Log in one tap

Tap a preset to record. Adjust your daily goal and three preset amounts whenever you need.

See your progress

Check today's totals, basic statistics, and a gentle review of the past seven days.

Correct mistakes

Delete a mistaken entry from today's records. On Mac, log quickly from the menu bar.

Your records stay yours

SipLog requires no separate account and includes no third-party ads or analytics. Records are stored on your device and can sync through your private Apple CloudKit database.
