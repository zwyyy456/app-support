Privacy policy

How SipLog handles your data

Last updated September 23, 2026

SipLog, provided by Changjun Zheng, helps you track water, standing, and optional kegel exercises. This policy explains how the current version handles those records.

Your records

Daily goals, preset settings, and the time and amount of each entry are stored on your device. Statistics and weekly reviews are calculated from those entries. SipLog does not require a separate product account.

iCloud sync

When you use iCloud, Apple CloudKit stores relevant data in the private database associated with your Apple ID and syncs it across your devices. The developer does not operate a server that receives these records and cannot read their contents through a developer-operated server. Apple's policies also apply to data processed by iCloud.

Collection and tracking

The current version contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not use data for cross-app tracking or send your records, device identifiers, or usage information to the developer.

Deletion and control

You can delete a mistaken entry from the current day in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from that device. Data synced to iCloud may remain there and can be managed in your Apple device's iCloud settings. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete iCloud data.
