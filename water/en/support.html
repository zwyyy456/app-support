Questions or feedback? Email zwyyy456@foxmail.com. It helps to include your device model, system and app versions, steps to reproduce, and a screenshot if useful. Please hide private records in screenshots.

Logging and corrections

On Today, choose a goal and tap a preset to log its amount. Use Adjust to change the daily goal and three presets. On iPhone and iPad, open Records from Today to delete a mistaken entry from the current day. On Mac, delete it in the Today panel. The Mac menu bar offers quick logging.

Standing and kegels

Water is always available. Standing starts enabled and kegels start hidden. You can change their visibility in Settings. Hiding a goal does not delete its history.

iCloud sync

Records are saved locally first. If your devices use the same Apple ID and have iCloud enabled for SipLog, your data can sync through your private CloudKit database. Sync may take time. Check your network, iCloud sign-in, and app iCloud permissions if it seems delayed.

Privacy

SipLog does not require a separate product account. Read the privacy policy for details about local storage and iCloud sync.
