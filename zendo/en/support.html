Zendo support

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Zendo helps you record where your time goes, understand its value, and review your allocation. If you need help, contact us by email.

Log your first interval

In Today, enter the activity you just finished, confirm its end time, and choose Invest, Maintain, Recover, or Drain. Save the entry. Subsequent entries pick up where the previous one ended, with no timer to start in advance. If you are in a hurry, save the interval and add its details later.

Find and review your entries

In History, select a date range, search activities or notes, and filter by activity, value category, or incomplete entries. In Statistics, explore value breakdowns, trends, top activities, and period comparisons.

What to include in a report

  • Your device model and operating system version
  • Your Zendo version
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • A screenshot or recording, if helpful. Hide personal time entries and other sensitive information first.

Privacy and sync

Zendo does not require a product account. When iCloud is enabled, your ledger syncs across your devices through your private iCloud database. Changes may take time to appear. Check that your devices use the same Apple ID and that iCloud access is enabled for Zendo.

Backups and restoration

Importing a backup restores your ledger from that file. Before restoring, check that you have selected the correct backup. Keep backup files secure: they may contain personal time entries and notes.
