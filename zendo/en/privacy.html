Zendo privacy policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Zendo is a personal time ledger provided by Hyperseek. This policy explains how Zendo handles information related to your use of the app.

1. Ledger data

Your time entries, activities, notes, value categories, goals, reviews, and app settings are your personal data. Zendo stores this data locally on your device and does not require a product account.

2. iCloud and CloudKit

When iCloud sync is enabled, Zendo uses Apple CloudKit to sync ledger data to the private iCloud database associated with your Apple ID, so you can use it across your own devices. The developer does not collect your ledger contents through developer-operated servers.

3. Local backups

Zendo supports exporting and restoring local ledger backups. Where a backup is stored and how it is transferred or shared depend on the storage location and actions you choose. Keep backup files containing personal time entries and notes secure.

4. Purchase status

If you purchase Zendo Pro, Apple processes the transaction through the App Store and StoreKit. Zendo reads only the purchase status needed to unlock features and does not receive your full payment information.

5. What we do not do

  • Use third-party advertising or analytics services.
  • Sell your personal information.
  • Require a Zendo product account.
  • Collect your ledger contents through developer-operated servers.

6. Deleting data

You can delete local ledger data in the app’s data management settings. When CloudKit sync is enabled, deletion may also sync to your private iCloud database. You can also manage the associated data through your system’s iCloud settings.

7. Third-party services

Zendo uses Apple services such as iCloud, CloudKit, the App Store, and StoreKit. Data handling by those services is also governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

8. Contact us

For questions about this policy or data handling, email zwyyy456@foxmail.com.
